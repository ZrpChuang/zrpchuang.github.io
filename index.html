Publications

P-DPO: Grounding Hallucination in Perceptual Processing via Calibration Direct Preference Optimization
Ruipeng Zhang, Zhihao Li, Haozhang Yuan, C. L. Philip Chen, Tong Zhang
ICLR 2026 Poster
PDF / BibTeX
Keywords: LLMs, MLLMs, Hallucination, DPO
This work proposes Perceptual Processing Direct Preference Optimization, a training paradigm that constructs on-policy preference pairs targeting focus-and-enhance perception and visual robustness. It further introduces a calibration loss to align visual signals with causal text generation, improving hallucination mitigation and robustness under degraded visual inputs.

Steer Where It Matters: Token-Level Visual-Sensitivity Steering for LVLMs Hallucination Mitigation
Ruipeng Zhang, Zhihao Li, C. L. Philip Chen, Tong Zhang
ICML 2026 Submission
PDF / Code / Project Page
Keywords: LVLMs, LLMs, Hallucination, Activation Steering, Representation Learning
This work proposes Token-Level Visual-Sensitivity Steering, a lightweight and plug-and-play method for hallucination mitigation in large vision-language models. The method extracts token-level steering vectors and applies visual-sensitivity-adaptive intervention during autoregressive decoding, selectively suppressing hallucination-prone spans while preserving evidence-grounded content.
